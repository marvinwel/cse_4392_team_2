TSI Restricted Courses
TSI RESTRICTED COURSES
If you are not complete in English and/or Math, there are specific courses that you
cannot take while enrolled in your required TSI coursework. The courses below are
the restricted courses for each area.
[TABLE: tab-separated cells]
TSI Not Complete Area	Restricted Classes
Math	CHEM (any class), ECON 2305, ECON 2306, MATH 1301 or higher (except for required TSI Math class), PHYS (any class)
English	ENGL 1301 (except when taking as required TSI English class), ENGL 1302, HIST 1301, HIST 1302, HIST 1331, HIST 1332, POLS 2311, POLS 2312, PSYC 1315, SOCI 1311, SOCI 2312
[END TABLE]
